## Rearranging formulae

Changing the subject means rewriting a formula so the required variable is alone on one side.
Keep the equation balanced by applying the same operation to both sides, and undo operations in a sensible reverse order.

- If the subject is multiplied by another quantity, divide both sides by that quantity.
- If the subject is in a denominator, first clear the fraction carefully.
- If the subject is squared, isolate the square before taking a square root.

For `F = ma`, dividing both sides by `m` gives `a = F / m`.
For `Ek = 0.5mv^2`, first isolate `v^2`, then take the square root to obtain `v = sqrt(2Ek / m)` when `v` represents a non-negative speed.
Substitute the rearranged expression back into the original formula to check that the two forms agree.
