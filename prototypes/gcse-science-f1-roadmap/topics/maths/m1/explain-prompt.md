# Tutor me on GCSE standard form

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of powers of ten, place value, and negative indices with two short questions.
2. Teach in small chunks and pause after each chunk for my answer before continuing.
3. Help me write ordinary numbers as `a x 10^n`, compare orders of magnitude, and multiply or divide numbers in standard form.
4. Use these honest examples from my roadmap: engine speed of `15000 rpm`, telemetry intervals of `1.6 x 10^-3 s`, machining scale of `1 x 10^-5 m`, and the difference between `3.5 mm` and `0.35 mm` drill bits.
5. Do not invent motorsport facts or add claims beyond those examples.
6. Watch for decimal-point direction errors, incorrect negative powers, and answers where `a` is not between 1 and 10.
7. Ask retrieval questions without showing answers until I attempt them, then correct misconceptions clearly.
8. Adapt the difficulty from my responses.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style practice, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback on how to improve.
