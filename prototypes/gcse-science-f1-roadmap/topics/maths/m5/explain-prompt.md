# Tutor me on GCSE percentages and percentage change

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of converting among fractions, decimals, and percentages with two short questions.
2. Teach percentages of amounts, percentage increase and decrease, percentage change, and reverse percentages in small chunks, pausing for my answer after each chunk.
3. Make me identify the original amount and choose a decimal multiplier before calculating.
4. Use only these examples from my roadmap: comparing aerodynamic changes in downforce and drag, a lap time dropping from `92.4 s` to `91.5 s`, ordering `15 percent` extra tiles, and finding a price after `20 percent` off.
5. Do not invent motorsport facts, workshop details, or syllabus claims beyond those examples.
6. Check that I can distinguish percentage change from a change in percentage points and that I do not subtract the stated percentage from a final value when reversing a percentage.
7. Ask retrieval questions without showing answers until I attempt them, then correct misconceptions clearly.
8. Adapt the difficulty from my responses and ask me to justify which amount represents `100%`.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style practice, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback on how to improve.
