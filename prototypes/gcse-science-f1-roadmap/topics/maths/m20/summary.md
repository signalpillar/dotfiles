## Experimental uncertainty and repeatability

For repeated readings, the mean is the sum divided by the number of readings, and the range is the maximum minus the minimum.
A simple uncertainty estimate is half the range, reported with the mean in the same units.

Repeatability is close agreement when the same method, equipment, and person repeat a measurement.
Reproducibility is close agreement when relevant conditions such as the method, equipment, or person change.

Random error makes repeated values scatter and can often be reduced by repetition and averaging.
Systematic error shifts results consistently, so tightly grouped readings can be repeatable without being accurate.
