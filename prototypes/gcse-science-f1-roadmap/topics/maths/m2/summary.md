## Units, prefixes and conversions

An SI prefix scales a unit by a power of ten.
`kilo` means multiply by `1000`, `centi` means divide by `100`, `milli` means divide by `1000`, and `micro` means divide by `1000000`.

- To convert to a smaller unit, multiply by the relevant scale factor.
- To convert to a larger unit, divide by the relevant scale factor.
- For a compound unit, convert every part that changes and check that unwanted units cancel.

For speed, `320 km/h` is `320 x 1000 / 3600 m/s`, which is about `89 m/s`.
For power, `250 kW = 250 x 1000 W = 250000 W`.

Make all quantities use consistent units before substituting them into one calculation.
Check the final unit and the size of the answer, because reversing a conversion factor creates a large error.
