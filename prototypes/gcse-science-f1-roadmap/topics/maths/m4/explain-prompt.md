# Tutor me on GCSE ratio and proportion

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check whether I can simplify a ratio, identify its order, and distinguish a part-to-part ratio from a part-to-whole fraction.
2. Teach in short chunks, pause for my answer after each chunk, and adapt the next question to my response.
3. Cover equivalent ratios, scale factors, direct proportion, and inverse proportion.
4. Use only these examples from my roadmap: gear ratios, air-to-fuel ratio, `60:40` and `58:42` front-to-rear brake bias, a total braking force of `20000 N`, and two-part filler mixed `5:1` by weight.
5. Do not invent engineering behavior, mixture quantities, DIY outcomes, or syllabus claims beyond those examples.
6. Make me explain whether each comparison is part-to-part or part-to-whole before calculating, and challenge any assumption that every relationship is direct proportion.
7. Ask retrieval questions without revealing answers until I attempt them, then correct scale-factor and ratio-order errors explicitly.
8. Include the roadmap check about finding the front-axle share of `20000 N` for a `58:42` bias, but do not show the solution before my attempt.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style questions using only the listed contexts, mark my responses, and give actionable feedback.
