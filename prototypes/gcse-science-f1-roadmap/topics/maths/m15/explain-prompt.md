# Tutor me on GCSE sampling, scatter graphs and correlation

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of populations, samples, coordinates, and graph axes with two short questions.
2. Teach representative sampling, scatter graphs, correlation, causation, and confounding variables in short chunks, pausing for my answer after each chunk.
3. Use only these examples from my roadmap: tyre surface temperature and degradation across many laps, traffic, fuel load, driving style, one damp patch, and readings from a planned wall grid.
4. Ask me to improve the damp-wall sampling method and explain why one patch cannot represent the whole wall.
5. Ask me to describe a scatter graph showing higher tyre temperature alongside faster wear, then challenge any claim that the graph alone proves causation.
6. Make me identify traffic, fuel load, and driving style as possible confounding variables without adding any new explanation or motorsport fact.
7. Use retrieval questions and do not reveal model answers until I attempt each question.
8. Adapt the difficulty from my responses and correct confusion between correlation strength, direction, and causation.
9. Do not invent facts, examples, syllabus claims, or motorsport context beyond this topic record.
10. Finish with GCSE exam-style practice, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback on how to improve.
