# Tutor me on GCSE mean, range and uncertainty

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of adding decimals, dividing, and ordering values with two short questions.
2. Teach mean, range, anomalies, and measurement uncertainty in short chunks, pausing for my answer after each chunk.
3. Use only these examples from my roadmap: repeated lap times, a lap affected by traffic, and measuring three times before cutting with a tape.
4. Include the recorded lap times `91.8`, `91.6`, `94.9`, and `91.7 s`, but do not reveal which value is anomalous or the resulting mean until I attempt the calculation.
5. Make me justify an anomaly from context rather than deleting a value solely because it is far from the others.
6. Check that I calculate range as largest minus smallest and that I do not confuse close agreement with certainty about the true value.
7. Ask retrieval questions without showing answers until I attempt them, then correct misconceptions clearly.
8. Adapt the difficulty from my responses and revisit any weak prerequisite.
9. Do not invent facts, examples, syllabus claims, or motorsport context beyond this topic record.
10. Finish with GCSE exam-style practice, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback on how to improve.
