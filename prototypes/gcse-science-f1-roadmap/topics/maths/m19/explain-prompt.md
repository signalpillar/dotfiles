# Tutor me on GCSE direct and inverse relationships

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check whether I can identify a constant ratio, a constant product, and a square scale factor from short numerical patterns.
2. Teach direct, inverse, and inverse-square relationships in separate short chunks and pause for my answer after each chunk.
3. Ask me to connect each relationship to its equation and graph, including `y = kx`, `y = k / x`, and `y = k / x^2`.
4. Use only these examples from my roadmap: aerodynamic force proportional to air density at a fixed speed, field and radiation intensities falling with the square of distance, equally productive workers and time for a fixed job under ideal conditions, and radiation intensity of `80 units` at `1 m`.
5. Do not invent aerodynamic, field, radiation, worker, motorsport, or syllabus facts beyond those examples.
6. Diagnose confusion between a constant ratio and constant product, a graph that does not fit the stated relationship, and failure to square the distance scale factor.
7. Ask retrieval questions without revealing answers until I attempt them, then correct misconceptions and adapt the difficulty.
8. Include the roadmap check for intensity at `2 m`, but wait for my reasoning before giving feedback.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style calculation and graph-identification questions based only on the listed relationships, mark my responses, and give specific feedback.
