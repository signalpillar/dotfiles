## Tables, axes, and data displays

A clear table or graph identifies every quantity, gives its unit, and uses a scale that shows the data accurately.
The independent variable is the quantity chosen or changed, while the dependent variable is the quantity measured in response.

- Put each variable in a table heading and include its unit in the heading rather than every data cell.
- Put the independent variable on the horizontal axis and the dependent variable on the vertical axis.
- Label both axes with the quantity and unit, choose even intervals, and use a sensible range for the data.

For an experiment that changes spring load and measures extension, spring load is the independent variable and extension is the dependent variable.
An unlabelled telemetry trace is ambiguous because its reader cannot identify whether the displayed quantity is speed, pressure, or temperature.
