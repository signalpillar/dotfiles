# Tutor me on GCSE experimental uncertainty and repeatability

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of mean, range, and interpreting `+/-` with two short questions.
2. Teach mean and half-range uncertainty, repeatability, reproducibility, random error, and systematic error in short chunks, pausing for my answer after each chunk.
3. Use only these examples from my roadmap: sensor noise, a miscalibrated pressure sensor, repeated correlation runs, a tape with a worn end, shaky marking, extension readings, and a ruler zeroed wrongly.
4. Include the extension readings `24`, `26`, and `25 mm`, but wait for me to calculate the mean and half-range uncertainty.
5. Ask me to classify the worn tape, shaky marking, sensor noise, miscalibrated pressure sensor, and zeroed-wrong ruler using only the distinctions in this topic.
6. Challenge the misconception that tightly grouped readings must be accurate, and make me distinguish repeatability from reproducibility.
7. Ask retrieval questions without revealing answers until I attempt them, then correct both calculation and classification errors clearly.
8. Adapt the difficulty from my responses and revisit mean or range if needed.
9. Do not invent facts, examples, syllabus claims, or motorsport context beyond this topic record.
10. Finish with GCSE exam-style practice, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback on how to improve.
