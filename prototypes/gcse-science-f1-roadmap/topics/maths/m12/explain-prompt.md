# Tutor me on GCSE probability

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of fractions, decimals, percentages, and the probability scale with two short questions.
2. Teach single and combined event probabilities in short chunks, pausing for my answer after each chunk.
3. Use only these examples from my roadmap: a strategy decision, the chance of a safety car in the next ten laps, drilling into a wall after using a detector, rain, and a safety car.
4. Include the stated safety-car probability of `40 percent` and the check where rain has probability `0.3` and a safety car has probability `0.4`.
5. Before allowing multiplication, ask me whether the events are stated to be independent and why that matters.
6. Make me distinguish the probability of both events from the probability of either event and connect a calculated probability to a cautious decision.
7. Do not reveal numerical answers until I attempt them, then diagnose errors in converting forms or combining events.
8. Adapt the difficulty from my responses and use retrieval questions to revisit weak points.
9. Do not invent facts, examples, syllabus claims, or motorsport context beyond this topic record.
10. Finish with GCSE exam-style practice, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback on how to improve.
