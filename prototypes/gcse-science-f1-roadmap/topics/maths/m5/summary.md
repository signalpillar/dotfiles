## Percentages and percentage change

A percentage expresses an amount out of `100`, so `15% = 0.15` and `120% = 1.20`.

- Find a percentage of an amount by multiplying by its decimal form.
- Increase by `p%` by multiplying by `1 + p/100`.
- Decrease by `p%` by multiplying by `1 - p/100`.
- Find percentage change with `change / original x 100%`.
- Reverse a percentage by dividing the final amount by the multiplier that produced it.

A lap time falling from `92.4 s` to `91.5 s` changes by `0.9 s`.
The percentage decrease is `0.9 / 92.4 x 100%`, which is about `0.97%`.

Always divide by the original amount, not the final amount.
A change between two percentage values can be stated in percentage points, which is different from calculating percentage change.
