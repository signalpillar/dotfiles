# Energy stores, conservation and efficiency

Energy can be held in stores and transferred between stores by pathways.
A complete description names the initial and final stores and the transfer pathway rather than saying that energy is simply used up.
In a closed system, total energy is conserved, although some may be transferred to less useful stores.

Efficiency compares useful output with total input:

`efficiency = useful energy transferred / total energy supplied`

The same ratio can use useful and total power when the quantities are compared consistently.
Multiply the ratio by `100%` for percentage efficiency.
Efficiency cannot exceed `100%`, and an output is useful only in relation to the intended job.

Friction braking transfers kinetic energy mainly to thermal stores, while regenerative braking can transfer some to a battery store.
LED and halogen lighting can provide the same intended output with different proportions transferred usefully and thermally.
