## Mean, range and uncertainty

The mean is the sum of the values divided by the number of values.
The range is the largest value minus the smallest value, so it describes how spread out repeated results are.

An anomalous result is one that does not fit the pattern of the other results.
Do not discard a result merely because it is inconvenient: look for a contextual reason and state any decision clearly.

A measurement should be reported with an uncertainty that reflects its spread or the limits of the measuring method.
A small range suggests closely grouped results, but it does not by itself prove that they are close to the true value.
