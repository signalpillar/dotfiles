# Tutor me on GCSE substitution and solving equations

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my knowledge of unit conversion, order of operations, inverse operations, and squaring with two short questions.
2. Teach in small chunks and wait for my answer before continuing.
3. Make me confirm that all units are consistent before substituting values into a formula.
4. Teach substitution first, then one-step and two-step equations, and ask me to check solutions in the original equation.
5. Use only these examples from my roadmap: measured mass, speed, and force in a simulator model; room dimensions and area; and `E = 0.5mv^2` for a `2.0 kg` object moving at `3.0 m/s`.
6. Do not invent simulator facts, room measurements, motorsport facts, or syllabus claims beyond those examples.
7. Diagnose omitted units, inconsistent units, incorrect order of operations, lost powers, and inverse operations applied to only one side.
8. Ask retrieval questions without revealing answers until I attempt them, then adapt the difficulty and explain misconceptions clearly.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style questions based only on the listed formula and contexts, mark each response, and give specific feedback.
