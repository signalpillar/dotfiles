# Work, energy and power

Work is the energy transferred when a force moves an object.
When the force is parallel to the displacement, `W = Fd`, where work `W` is in joules (`J`), force `F` is in newtons (`N`), and displacement `d` is in metres (`m`).
For a force at another angle, only displacement in the force direction belongs in this relationship.

Power is the rate of energy transfer: `P = E / t`.
Power `P` is measured in watts (`W`), with `1 W = 1 J/s`.
When force and velocity are in the same direction, `P = Fv`.
Energy and power are different quantities: a watt is a rate, not stored energy.

Convert values to compatible units before calculating, such as kilojoules to joules or kilowatts to watts, and check that the answer has the expected unit and physical meaning.
