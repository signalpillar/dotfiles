## Significant figures and sensible precision

Significant figures count meaningful digits from the first non-zero digit, wherever the decimal point lies.
Decimal places instead count positions to the right of the decimal point.

To round to a chosen number of significant figures, keep the required digits, inspect the next digit, and round the last kept digit up when the next digit is `5` or more.
Keep placeholder zeros when they are needed to preserve the value's size.

A calculated speed of `27.638419 m/s` based on data recorded to three significant figures should be reported as `27.6 m/s`.
The calculator's extra digits do not make the measurements more precise.

Choose precision that the measuring instrument and input data can support.
A tape measure marked in millimetres cannot justify reporting a cut to the nearest micrometre.
