# Tutor me on GCSE Pythagoras and trigonometry

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as one long explanation.

1. First check whether I can identify the hypotenuse, square and square-root numbers, label opposite and adjacent sides relative to an angle, and read a straight-line graph and its gradient as required by M6.
2. Teach Pythagoras and right-angled sine, cosine, and tangent, including inverse trigonometric functions for finding an unknown angle, in short chunks, pausing for my answer after each chunk.
3. Make me choose the method before calculating, and ask me to explain why that method fits.
4. Use only these roadmap examples: a wing force of `5000 N` acting at `20 degrees` to the vertical, the `3, 4, 5` triangle square-corner check, and finding a roof pitch.
5. Do not invent motorsport, building, or syllabus facts beyond those examples.
6. Check that I identify sides relative to the stated angle and that my calculator is in degree mode.
7. Probe mistakes such as treating a non-hypotenuse side as the hypotenuse, swapping opposite and adjacent, or using Pythagoras without a right angle.
8. Ask retrieval questions without revealing answers until I attempt them, then adapt the difficulty to my responses.
9. Finish with GCSE-style practice that includes finding unknown sides and angles using only the established examples, wait for each attempt, mark it, and give specific feedback.
