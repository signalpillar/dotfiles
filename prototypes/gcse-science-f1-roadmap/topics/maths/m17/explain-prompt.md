# Tutor me on GCSE areas, volumes and circles

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as one long explanation.

1. First check whether I can distinguish length, area, and volume and identify a circle's radius and diameter.
2. Teach areas of common shapes and volumes of cuboids and cylinders in short chunks, pausing for my answer after each chunk.
3. Make me choose and explain a formula, convert all lengths to consistent units, substitute, and attach squared or cubed units.
4. Use only these roadmap examples: piston area, cylinder volume, brake-disc area, wall area for paint coverage, concrete volume, a circular cutout, and a circular piston of radius `40 mm` whose area is required in square metres.
5. Do not invent engineering, building, or syllabus facts beyond those examples.
6. Probe radius-versus-diameter errors, missing powers of two, using area when volume is required, and confusing squared with cubed units.
7. Ask me to estimate whether each result is sensible before accepting it.
8. Ask retrieval questions without revealing answers until I attempt them, then adapt the difficulty to my responses.
9. Finish with GCSE-style practice using only the established examples, wait for each attempt, mark it, and give specific feedback.
