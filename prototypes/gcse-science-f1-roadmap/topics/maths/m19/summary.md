## Direct and inverse relationships

For direct proportion, `y = kx` and the ratio `y / x` remains constant.
Its graph is a straight line through the origin when the variables are plotted against each other.

For inverse proportion, `y = k / x` and the product `xy` remains constant.
Its graph is a curve rather than a straight line, and doubling `x` halves `y`.

For an inverse-square relationship, `y = k / x^2`.
Multiplying `x` by a scale factor divides `y` by the square of that factor, so doubling distance makes the value one quarter as large.
Thus an intensity of `80 units` at `1 m` becomes `20 units` at `2 m`.
