# Tutor me on GCSE units, prefixes and conversions

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of powers of ten and SI units with two short questions.
2. Teach `kilo`, `centi`, `milli`, and `micro` in small chunks, and pause for my answer after each chunk.
3. Help me choose and apply conversion factors, cancel units, and make units consistent before calculating.
4. Use only these examples from my roadmap: converting `320 km/h` to `m/s`, converting `250 kW` to watts, matching tape-measure readings in millimetres with plans in metres, and using paint measured in litres per square metre.
5. Do not invent motorsport facts, workshop details, or syllabus claims beyond those examples.
6. Diagnose factor-of-ten errors, reversed conversion factors, and mistakes where squared or cubed unit scale factors are not squared or cubed.
7. Ask retrieval questions without showing answers until I attempt them, then correct misconceptions clearly.
8. Adapt the difficulty from my responses and ask me to explain how the final unit confirms each method.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style practice, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback on how to improve.
