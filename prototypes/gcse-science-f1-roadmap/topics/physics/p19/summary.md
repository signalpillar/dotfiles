# Springs, Hooke's law and elastic energy

Extension is the change from a spring's original length, not its total length.
Within the proportional region, force and extension obey:

`F = kx`

Here `F` is force in newtons, `k` is spring stiffness in newtons per metre, and `x` is extension or compression in metres.
If a restoring-force direction is required, it acts opposite to the displacement; the scalar equation uses force magnitude.

The limit of proportionality is where force stops being directly proportional to extension, so the force-extension graph stops being a straight line through the origin.
It is not the same definition as the elastic limit, beyond which the spring does not fully return to its original shape.

For a spring obeying Hooke's law, elastic potential energy is:

`E = 1/2 kx^2`

Energy is measured in joules.
Convert millimetres to metres before substituting when `k` is in `N/m`, then check the answer's unit and size.
