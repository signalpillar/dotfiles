## Standard form

Standard form writes a number as `a x 10^n`, where `1 <= a < 10` and `n` is an integer.
Move the decimal point until the first part is between 1 and 10, then count the places moved.

- Large numbers use positive powers: `15000 = 1.5 x 10^4`.
- Small numbers use negative powers: `0.0016 = 1.6 x 10^-3` and `0.00001 = 1 x 10^-5`.
- A larger power of ten usually means a larger order of magnitude, so `3 x 10^-4` is greater than `8 x 10^-5`.

When multiplying, multiply the first numbers and add the powers.
When dividing, divide the first numbers and subtract the powers.
Convert the result back so its first number is at least 1 and less than 10.
