## Surface area to volume ratio

Surface area measures the total exposed area of an object, while volume measures the space inside it.
Calculate both in consistent units, then divide surface area by volume to obtain `SA / V`.

For a cube of side `s`, surface area is `6s^2` and volume is `s^3`, so `SA / V = 6 / s`.
A `2 cm` cube has surface area `24 cm^2`, volume `8 cm^3`, and ratio `3 cm^-1`.
A `4 cm` cube has surface area `96 cm^2`, volume `64 cm^3`, and ratio `1.5 cm^-1`.

When equal shapes scale up, area grows with the square of length but volume grows with the cube, so the ratio decreases.
More surface area per unit volume allows a greater heat or mass transfer rate relative to volume when the other relevant conditions are unchanged.
