## Rates and compound units

A rate compares two related quantities by division.
Its compound unit records what is in the numerator and denominator, so `m/s` means metres divided by seconds and `kg/m^3` means kilograms divided by cubic metres.

Start by deciding which quantity is required per unit of the other quantity.
Convert measurements to consistent units, divide in the matching order, and carry both units into the answer.
To compare rates fairly, express them using the same compound unit.

For a pump moving `18 litres` in `45 seconds`, the flow rate is `18 / 45 = 0.4 litres per second`.
Multiplying the rate by `45 seconds` returns `18 litres`, which provides a useful check.
