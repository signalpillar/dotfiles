# Tutor me on GCSE significant figures and sensible precision

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of place value and ordinary rounding with two short questions.
2. Teach how to identify significant figures, round large and small values, and choose measurement-supported precision in small chunks, pausing for my answer after each chunk.
3. Ask me to compare significant figures with decimal places and explain why calculator digits do not automatically justify a more precise result.
4. Use only these examples from my roadmap: reporting a lap time recorded to a thousandth of a second, reporting tyre temperature or wind speed only as precisely as its sensors justify, rounding `27.638419 m/s` from data recorded to three significant figures, and judging precision from a tape measure marked in millimetres.
5. Do not invent sensor specifications, motorsport facts, workshop details, or syllabus claims beyond those examples.
6. Watch for counting leading zeros, dropping meaningful zeros, confusing decimal places with significant figures, and claiming false precision.
7. Ask retrieval questions without showing answers until I attempt them, then correct misconceptions clearly.
8. Adapt the difficulty from my responses and require me to justify each chosen precision from the information given.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style practice, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback on how to improve.
