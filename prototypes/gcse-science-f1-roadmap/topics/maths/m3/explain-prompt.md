# Tutor me on GCSE rearranging formulae

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of inverse operations, fractions, squares, square roots, and consistent units with two short questions.
2. Teach in small chunks and pause for my answer after each chunk.
3. Show me how to change the subject when a formula contains products, fractions, or a squared variable while keeping both sides balanced.
4. Use only these examples from my roadmap: rearranging `F = ma` to find acceleration, rearranging `Ek = 0.5mv^2` to find speed, and rearranging `area = length x width` to find the missing decking width.
5. Do not invent values, motorsport facts, DIY facts, or syllabus claims beyond those examples.
6. Diagnose mistakes involving the wrong inverse operation, changing only one side, dividing by the wrong factor, or forgetting the square root.
7. Ask retrieval questions without revealing answers until I attempt them, then explain any misconception clearly.
8. Adapt the difficulty to my responses and ask me to verify a rearrangement by substitution.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style rearrangement questions based only on the listed formulae, wait for my attempts, mark each response, and give specific feedback.
