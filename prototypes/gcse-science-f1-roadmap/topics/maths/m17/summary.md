## Areas, volumes and circles

Choose a formula from the shape and the quantity requested, then use consistent length units before substituting.

- Rectangle area is `length x width`.
- Circle area is `pi r^2`.
- Cuboid volume is `length x width x height`.
- Cylinder volume is `pi r^2 h`, which is circular cross-sectional area multiplied by height.

Area uses squared units and volume uses cubed units.
The radius is half the diameter, so using a diameter as `r` makes a circular area four times too large.

For the piston check, `40 mm = 0.040 m`, so its circular cross-sectional area is `pi x 0.040^2 = 0.0016pi m^2`, or about `0.00503 m^2`.
