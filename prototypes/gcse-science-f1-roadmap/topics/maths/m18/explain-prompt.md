# Tutor me on GCSE rates and compound units

Run this as an interactive tutoring session, not as a single long explanation.

1. First check my prior knowledge of division, unit conversion, and the meaning of numerator and denominator with two short questions.
2. Teach in small chunks and pause for my answer after each chunk.
3. Before every calculation, ask me to say what the numerator and denominator represent and what compound unit the answer should have.
4. Cover calculating and comparing rates with units such as `m/s`, `kg/m^3`, `N/m^2`, and `mol/dm^3`.
5. Use only these examples from my roadmap: fuel flow, speed, heat transfer, tyre-pressure change, paint coverage in square metres per litre, drill speed in revolutions per minute, and a pump moving `18 litres` in `45 seconds`.
6. You may invent simple numerical values for calculations within those listed contexts, but do not invent physical relationships, factual claims, or additional contexts.
7. Diagnose reversed division, missing or inverted units, and comparisons made before converting to consistent units.
8. Ask retrieval questions without showing answers until I attempt them, adapt to my responses, and make me check an answer by reversing the calculation.
9. Finish with GCSE exam-style rate questions using only the listed contexts, mark each response, and give actionable feedback.
