# Chemistry Resource Publication Plan

## Outcome

Publish complete support bundles for C1 through C33 while preserving every published M1-M20 and P1-P35 bundle and its catalog mapping byte for byte.
The finished chemistry catalog will contain all 33 manifest chemistry topics, and every topic will have a concise board-neutral summary, an interactive tutor prompt, at least two reviewed free supporting resources, and one separate focused English Wikipedia link.
This slice will add chemistry content and catalog mappings only, without changing the embedded manifest, its 121 topic records or 189 prerequisite edges, `papers.json`, runtime code, validators, audit tooling, fixtures, or existing tests.

## Authority And Safety Boundaries

The embedded manifest remains authoritative for every C1-C33 ID, title, specification sentence, prerequisite edge, check, stage, driver, and F1 or DIY example.
The bundle prose may explain the manifest scope but must not copy manifest-only fields into `topic.json` or claim additional exam-board content.
The final live destination after every redirect remains authoritative for resource title, publisher, subject coverage, access conditions, and link metadata.
Chemistry claims must be supported by inspected credible sources, and an evidence gap must leave the topic unpublished rather than invite a plausible invention.
The prose and prompt must distinguish simplified models from chemical reality where literal interpretation would create a misconception, including dot-and-cross diagrams, particle diagrams, equilibrium arrows, reactivity-series ordering, and idealised gas-volume assumptions.
Every calculation must show balanced equations or working, define units (g, mol, g/dm^3, mol/dm^3, dm^3), convert to compatible units before substitution, and check the final unit and chemical sense.
No resource may invent motorsport performance figures, engineering causation, medical advice, or practical methods beyond the exact manifest examples and inspected safe educational evidence.
Practical content covering acids and alkalis, electrolysis (including chlorine gas risk from aqueous chloride electrolysis), flame tests, gas tests, exothermic reactions, corrosive salt preparation, distillation, and heating must describe school-level planning and interpretation rather than instructing a learner to handle concentrated corrosive reagents, hazardous gases, naked flames, or hot apparatus without qualified supervision and an approved method and appropriate personal protective equipment.
No resource may give actionable instructions for producing, concentrating, or releasing a hazardous gas (chlorine, hydrogen in bulk) outside a supervised school laboratory context.

## Evidence Delegation And Independence

Assign each bounded batch below to one evidence researcher so related terminology, equations, models, and examples are checked consistently.
Require the researcher to read every assigned manifest record and produce an unpublished draft plus an evidence log for each topic under `/var/folders/f8/bb6ngcg16k5b6sd83h72xy880000gn/T/opencode/gcse-chemistry-resource-drafts/` so another session can resume without conversational context or an undisclosed temporary path.
Each evidence log must record the candidate URL, final HTTPS URL after redirects, page title, publisher, access and gate result, exact topic relevance, claims supported, limitations or model qualifications, research execution ID, draft execution ID, reviewer execution ID, review timestamp, and approval or rejection verdict.
Require at least four supporting-resource candidates and at least two Wikipedia candidates per topic so rejection does not force weak publication choices.
Assign every completed topic to a reviewer who did not research or draft that topic.
The independent reviewer must reopen every declared supporting destination plus the Wikipedia destination, compare prose and prompt line by line with the manifest and evidence log, run the topic gate, and approve or reject that topic separately.
No research or drafting execution may approve its own topic, no group-level approval may substitute for per-topic review, and a failing topic remains outside the published tree while passing siblings continue through review.
The listed F1 and DIY examples are the only permitted factual real-world contexts for a topic, but neutral abstract substances, diagrams, fictional datasets, and context-free numerical questions may be created when they add no factual or practical claim beyond the manifest scope.

## Batch Ordering Note

The batches below are ordered by chemistry-internal prerequisite depth rather than pure surface theme, so every topic's C-prefixed prerequisites are covered in an earlier or the same batch before it: C16 (acids, bases and salts) moves into Batch B because C21, C22, C23, C25, and C32 all depend on it, and grouping it with late "acids and equilibrium" topics would have forced researchers to write esterification, ion tests, water treatment, and salt-preparation content before acid chemistry existed anywhere in the library.
Cross-subject M and P prerequisites are already published and need no reordering.

## Bounded Research Batches

### Batch A: Foundational Structure, Mixtures And Quantities

- C1 has exact scope limited to atomic structure, isotopes, electron arrangement, and trends in groups and periods; its prerequisite diagnostic asks for no prior roadmap topic but checks that the learner can name protons, neutrons and electrons and locate atomic number and mass number on a symbol; its misconception checks target electrons occupying fixed circular orbits rather than shells, assuming all atoms of an element are identical rather than allowing isotopes, confusing mass number with atomic number, and UK Group 3 versus IUPAC Group 13 numbering; and its permitted factual contextual examples are aluminium being light and tungsten being dense because of what is inside their atoms, tungsten ballast concentrating mass in a small block, and a metal spec sheet only making sense once atomic behaviour is understood.
- C14 has exact scope limited to distinguishing elements, compounds and mixtures and explaining filtration, crystallisation, distillation and chromatography; its prerequisite diagnostic checks P16 particle model and changes of state; its misconception checks target believing a mixture must have fixed composition, believing filtering removes dissolved substances, believing distillation chemically changes the substances involved, and confusing dissolving with melting; and its permitted factual contextual examples are fuel, coolant and lubricants being mixtures whose composition matters and being separated by distillation and chromatography, and filtering paint removing solid debris while distilling a liquid separates substances with different boiling points.
- C2 has exact scope limited to ionic, covalent and metallic bonding and the properties each produces; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C1 atomic structure and electron arrangement; its misconception checks target assuming ionic bonding only describes table salt, assuming covalent bonding is always weak, describing metallic bonding as glue holding fixed atoms, and drawing a dot-and-cross diagram for every bond type regardless of structure; and its permitted factual contextual examples are metallic bonding explaining why aluminium alloys can be shaped and conduct heat, strongly bonded graphitic carbon fibres with fibre direction and polymer matrix setting composite stiffness, and glue, solder and a weld holding things together by three different mechanisms.
- C15 has exact scope limited to trends in Groups 1, 7 and 0 explained by electron structure and predicting properties from periodic position; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C1 electron arrangement and periodic position; its misconception checks target believing Group 0 elements readily react, assuming every element in a group behaves identically, assuming a trend reverses at an arbitrary boundary, and assuming same-group membership guarantees identical properties rather than a graded trend; and its permitted factual contextual examples are argon, a Group 0 gas, providing an inert atmosphere for precision welding and a TIG welder using inert shielding gas so hot metal does not react with air.
- C4 has exact scope limited to relative formula mass, the mole, and using balanced equations to calculate reacting masses; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C1 formulae and M4 ratio and proportion; its misconception checks target treating moles as a count of molecules without a defined particle number, believing mass changes during a reaction, believing balancing an equation changes a substance's formula, and assuming one mole always occupies 24 dm^3 regardless of state or conditions; and its permitted factual contextual examples are fuel flow measured by mass and conservation of mass applying to fuel plus oxygen entering an engine against all products leaving it, and two-part epoxy needing exact quantities because excess hardener sets weaker rather than faster.

### Batch B: Acids, Structure-Derived Materials, Rates And Electrolysis

- C16 has exact scope limited to the pH scale, neutralisation, and preparing named salts from acids reacting with bases, carbonates and metals; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C1 formulae and C14 distinguishing substances and mixtures; its misconception checks target assuming acid always means corrosive and hazardous rather than a defined pH range, believing neutralisation destroys the acid rather than forming a salt and water, assuming salt always means sodium chloride, and assuming any indicator changes colour at any pH rather than at a specific range; and its permitted factual contextual examples are coolant and cleaning fluids being monitored for pH because acidic or alkaline contamination attacks metals, seals and coatings, and limescale remover being acidic and reacting with carbonate deposits.
- C3 has exact scope limited to relating giant covalent, simple molecular, metallic and nanoparticle structures to melting point, strength and conductivity; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C2 bonding types; its misconception checks target assuming diamond and graphite differ in bonding type rather than only in structural arrangement, confusing giant and simple molecular structures, assuming allotropes must be different elements, and assuming any polymer is one giant covalent structure; and its permitted factual contextual examples are a carbon-fibre chassis chosen for stiffness and low weight, titanium suspension pickups staying strong when hot and loaded, and MDF, plywood and hardwood looking interchangeable until loaded, when structure decides everything.
- C5 has exact scope limited to explaining reaction rate through collision frequency and activation energy and describing the effect of catalysts; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C2 bonding and particle behaviour; its misconception checks target believing a catalyst is consumed in the reaction, treating rate as only how fast a reaction looks rather than a measurable quantity, treating concentration as the only factor affecting rate, and assuming every particle collision results in a reaction; and its permitted factual contextual examples are fuel needing to burn within a few thousandths of a second inside a cylinder, compression raising the collision rate, a catalytic converter speeding up exhaust clean-up, and paint drying faster in a warm room because particles have more energy and meet more often.
- C7 has exact scope limited to electrolysis of molten and aqueous compounds, predicting products, and writing half equations; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C2 ionic bonding and ion formation; its misconception checks target believing electrons flow through the electrolyte itself rather than ions migrating while electrons flow in the external circuit, assuming the cathode is always the negative electrode regardless of cell type, treating molten and aqueous electrolysis as producing identical products, and describing ion migration without linking it to the completed circuit; and its permitted factual contextual examples are aluminium parts being anodised, an electrolysis process that grows a hard oxide layer, aluminium being extracted by electrolysis in the first place, and electroplating a bike part or lifting rust from an old tool by electrolysis at home.
- C31 has exact scope limited to planning and carrying out filtration, crystallisation, distillation and paper chromatography including Rf measurement; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C14 separation methods and M16 tables, axes and data displays; its misconception checks target drawing the pencil baseline in soluble ink or below the solvent level, treating the solvent-front position alone as the result rather than a calculated ratio, calculating Rf as the raw distance a spot travelled rather than a ratio of distances, and relying on one uncontrolled sample instead of a comparison spot; and its permitted factual contextual examples are fuel analysis producing a chemical fingerprint by separating a mixture with controlled samples and calibrated distances, and separating pigment from ink on paper showing that one visible colour can contain several dissolved substances.

### Batch C: Materials, Energy Changes, Reactivity And Fuels

- C10 has exact scope limited to describing addition and condensation polymers and explaining how a composite combines two materials; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C3 giant, simple and composite structures; its misconception checks target assuming polymer means plastic specifically, treating addition and condensation polymerisation as the same mechanism, assuming a composite can be any mixture of materials, and confusing thermosetting with thermoplastic behaviour; and its permitted factual contextual examples are carbon fibre being a composite where stiff fibres carry load and an epoxy polymer holds them in shape, and a fibreglass repair kit using the same glass-mat-plus-resin idea that fails if either part is skimped.
- C11 has exact scope limited to explaining why alloys are harder than pure metals and describing rusting and methods of corrosion prevention; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C3 structure-property links; its misconception checks target treating an alloy as a simple physical mixture rather than a structure with disrupted layers, assuming the hardest available alloy is always the best choice, treating rust as a synonym for all corrosion, and assuming only iron and steel can corrode; and its permitted factual contextual examples are titanium and steel alloys chosen for hot, heavily loaded parts with coatings preventing corrosion between races, and galvanised screws protecting steel outdoors because zinc corrodes sacrificially first.
- C6 has exact scope limited to classifying reactions as exothermic or endothermic, drawing reaction profiles, and calculating energy change from bond energies; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C5 collision theory and activation energy; its misconception checks target assuming exothermic only means producing heat rather than any energy release, confusing activation energy with the overall energy change, believing breaking bonds releases energy, and believing forming bonds absorbs energy; and its permitted factual contextual examples are combustion being strongly exothermic, which is why so much of a car is cooling system rather than engine, and a tub of curing epoxy warming in your hand as an exothermic reaction you can feel.
- C18 has exact scope limited to using the reactivity series to predict displacement, extraction and oxidation-reduction reactions; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C2 bonding and C15 periodic trends; its misconception checks target believing carbon can reduce any metal oxide regardless of relative reactivity, treating electrolysis as the only extraction method for every metal, defining oxidation only as oxygen gain rather than more general electron loss, and assuming a metal's reactivity is identical against every other substance; and its permitted factual contextual examples are aluminium needing electrolysis for extraction because it is more reactive than carbon while less reactive metals can be recovered by chemical reduction, and a more reactive zinc coating protecting steel by oxidising first even where the coating is scratched.
- C9 has exact scope limited to describing alkanes, the products of complete and incomplete combustion, fractional distillation and cracking; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C4 balanced equations and conservation of mass; its misconception checks target treating all hydrocarbons as chemically identical, believing incomplete combustion only produces carbon monoxide rather than also soot and other partial-oxidation products, assuming saturated hydrocarbons are unreactive, and assuming a shorter carbon chain always makes a worse fuel; and its permitted factual contextual examples are every car from 2026 running fully sustainable fuel made from non-food biomass or captured carbon rather than extracted crude, and a smoking petrol strimmer running rich as visible incomplete combustion.

### Batch D: Cells, Analysis, Atmosphere And Transition Metals

- C8 has exact scope limited to describing simple cells, rechargeable cells and hydrogen fuel cells and comparing them; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C7 electrolysis and half equations and P10 current, potential difference and resistance; its misconception checks target believing a cell creates energy rather than converting stored chemical energy, assuming recharging exactly reverses the original chemistry with no losses, treating a fuel cell as functionally identical to a battery, and assuming voltage depends only on physical cell size; and its permitted factual contextual examples are an F1 energy store having a mandated maximum mass, turning the whole design problem into joules per kilogram, and rechargeable torch cells costing more up front but winning over a year of use.
- C12 has exact scope limited to distinguishing pure substances from mixtures, using Rf values, and describing tests for common gases and ions; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C9 hydrocarbon mixtures and separation context; its misconception checks target treating an Rf value as fixed regardless of solvent or conditions, believing the baseline may sit below the solvent level, assuming a single spot on any chromatogram proves purity, and assuming more spots always means more of a substance rather than more distinct substances; and its permitted factual contextual examples are every race-weekend fuel sample being checked by chromatography against the declared blend, and water-hardness strips and soil test kits using a known reaction to give a readable result.
- C13 has exact scope limited to describing the greenhouse effect, sources of atmospheric pollutants, and ways to reduce a carbon footprint; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C9 combustion products; its misconception checks target treating carbon dioxide as the only greenhouse gas, defining carbon footprint as direct emissions only, treating ozone depletion as equivalent to the greenhouse effect, and assuming renewable automatically means zero carbon across its whole life cycle; and its permitted factual contextual examples are most of a race team's footprint coming from freight and travel rather than the cars themselves, and loft insulation cutting heat loss, fuel burned, and carbon dioxide as one linked chain.
- C24 has exact scope limited to evaluating environmental impacts across raw materials, manufacture, use and disposal, including reuse and recycling; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C2 material origins and M5 percentages; its misconception checks target treating a life-cycle assessment as covering only environmental factors, assuming recycling is always the better outcome, assuming a life-cycle assessment always gives one unambiguous answer, and assuming the manufacture stage only involves an energy cost; and its permitted factual contextual examples are a lighter component needing a whole-life comparison because it may cut energy use in service but require energy-intensive manufacture, and a durable repair sometimes beating repeated cheap replacement on life-cycle impact.
- C28 has exact scope limited to comparing transition metals with Group 1 metals and describing variable ions, coloured compounds and catalytic uses; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C15 periodic trends and C2 bonding; its misconception checks target assuming every metal is a transition metal, assuming every transition-metal compound is coloured, assuming transition metals are always catalysts, and assuming reactivity is identical across a transition period; and its permitted factual contextual examples are iron, titanium and nickel alloys using transition-metal chemistry for strength, heat resistance and corrosion behaviour, and rust and copper-based wood treatments showing the coloured chemistry typical of transition metals.

### Batch E: Quantitative Chemistry And Practical Salts

- C27 has exact scope limited to calculating solution concentrations and reacting gas volumes, including molar gas volume at room conditions where required; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C4 moles and M18 rates and compound units; its misconception checks target treating concentration as a total amount rather than an amount per volume, treating mol/dm^3 and g/dm^3 as interchangeable, and assuming 24 dm^3 per mole applies regardless of temperature and pressure; and its permitted factual contextual examples are combustion and emissions calculations comparing amounts of gaseous reactants and products, and diluting a concentrated cleaner safely using volume and concentration ratios rather than an unmeasured splash.
- C26 has exact scope limited to calculating percentage yield and atom economy and identifying the limiting reactant in a process; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C4 moles and M5 percentages; its misconception checks target assuming the theoretical yield is always achievable in practice, treating atom economy as equivalent to percentage yield, believing a limiting reactant changes what product is formed rather than how much, and assuming 100 percent atom economy means zero waste of any kind; and its permitted factual contextual examples are materials suppliers wanting high yield and atom economy because unwanted products cost raw material, separation energy and disposal effort, and a resin kit with too little hardener being limited by the hardener rather than the resin.
- C20 has exact scope limited to describing cracking, testing for alkenes, and writing addition reactions that form polymers or other products; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C9 hydrocarbons and C2 covalent bonding; its misconception checks target treating cracking as breaking any bond rather than specifically longer hydrocarbon chains, describing an alkene as an alkane missing a hydrogen rather than a distinct unsaturated structure, treating addition as a replacement reaction, and treating the bromine-water test result as evidence of combustion; and its permitted factual contextual examples are cracking turning larger hydrocarbons into useful smaller fuels and reactive alkene feedstocks, and plastic storage boxes and cable insulators beginning as alkene monomers joined into long chains.
- C23 has exact scope limited to explaining how potable water is produced from fresh or salt water and outlining sewage and wastewater treatment; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C14 separation methods and C16 acids and neutralisation context; its misconception checks target assuming filtered water is automatically pure, assuming desalination is simple boiling, assuming all water sources need identical treatment, and assuming sewage treatment removes every contaminant completely; and its permitted factual contextual examples are temporary race facilities needing safe drinking water and managed wastewater for thousands of people, governed by the same treatment stages as any town, and a filter removing particles from rainwater while safe drinking water also needs treatment for dissolved substances and microbes.
- C32 has exact scope limited to preparing a soluble salt from an acid and an insoluble reactant using measured heating, filtration, evaporation and crystallisation; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C16 salt preparation and C31 separation technique; its misconception checks target believing any excess reactant is simply wasted rather than a deliberate way to ensure complete reaction, assuming crystallisation is always achieved purely by evaporation to dryness, assuming purity means completely free of water rather than free of unreacted solid, and filtering after crystallisation instead of before it to remove excess insoluble reactant; and its permitted factual contextual examples are laboratory preparation following a controlled sequence so a sample is pure enough for reliable testing, and growing clean crystals requiring insoluble material to be removed before the solution is concentrated and cooled.

### Batch F: Titration, Ion Tests, Organic Chemistry And Climate

- C17 has exact scope limited to carrying out an acid-alkali titration and calculating concentration in g/dm^3 and mol/dm^3; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C16 neutralisation and C27 concentration calculations and M20 experimental uncertainty and repeatability; its misconception checks target treating the indicator colour change alone as the quantitative result, treating a pipette and a burette as interchangeable pieces of apparatus, assuming a single reading is enough rather than requiring concordant results, and always reading the meniscus from the bottom regardless of whether the solution is colourless or coloured; and its permitted factual contextual examples are laboratory quality control measuring fluid composition precisely rather than by colour alone, and a pool-water test estimating concentration while a titration gives a quantitative result from a measured reacting volume.
- C22 has exact scope limited to using flame tests, precipitate tests, and tests for carbonate, halide, sulfate and common gases to identify substances; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C12 analysis techniques and C16 ions from acids and salts; its misconception checks target assuming a flame test gives the same colour for every compound of a metal, assuming a precipitate is always the same colour regardless of the ion present, skipping a confirmatory gas test and assuming no reaction means no gas, and assuming a positive carbonate test alone proves carbon dioxide without the limewater confirmation; and its permitted factual contextual examples are laboratories identifying fluid contamination through calibrated instrumental methods built on the same characteristic-signal idea, and a water-hardness kit detecting dissolved ions through a controlled colour change rather than taste or appearance.
- C21 has exact scope limited to describing reactions and uses of alcohols and carboxylic acids, fermentation, oxidation and ester formation; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C20 addition chemistry and C16 acids; its misconception checks target treating alcohols as useful only as fuel, treating oxidation of an alcohol as equivalent to combustion, treating esterification as a simple acid-base neutralisation rather than a condensation reaction, and treating carboxylic acids as strong mineral acids rather than weak acids; and its permitted factual contextual examples are ethanol and ester chemistry appearing in solvents, cleaners and material manufacture around engineering facilities, and alcohol solvents cleaning residues while esters contribute characteristic smells in paints and adhesives.
- C29 has exact scope limited to describing nanoparticle scale, high surface-area-to-volume ratio, applications, and uncertainties about risk; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C3 structure-property links and M11 surface area to volume ratio; its misconception checks target treating nano as meaning simply small rather than a defined size range, treating surface-area-to-volume ratio as an abstract number disconnected from reactivity, assuming every nanoparticle application is beneficial, and assuming nanoparticles share identical properties with the bulk material; and its permitted factual contextual examples are nanostructured additives and coatings altering friction, wear or surface behaviour while claims still need evidence about performance and exposure risk, and paints and sunscreens using nanoparticles for optical or surface properties that larger particles do not provide.
- C30 has exact scope limited to describing atmospheric development, greenhouse gases, climate evidence and its uncertainty, and major air pollutants; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C1 atomic and molecular composition and M15 sampling, scatter graphs and correlation; its misconception checks target assuming the early atmosphere resembled the present one, assuming oxygen was always present at current levels, assuming a carbon dioxide decrease proves combustion used it up rather than allowing for photosynthesis and ocean or rock absorption, and treating ice-core data as a direct temperature reading rather than a proxy; and its permitted factual contextual examples are motorsport emissions splitting into greenhouse gases from fuel and logistics versus local pollutants from combustion and transport needing different measurement and control, and a correctly vented heater reducing carbon monoxide risk while insulation reduces carbon-dioxide-linked energy use.

### Batch G: Equilibrium Applications And Rate Practicals

- C19 has exact scope limited to describing dynamic equilibrium and predicting the effects of concentration, pressure and temperature changes; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C5 collision theory, C6 energy changes, and C27 gas and concentration quantities; its misconception checks target believing the forward reaction stops at equilibrium rather than continuing at equal rate to the reverse, assuming equilibrium requires equal concentrations of reactants and products, believing a catalyst can shift the position of equilibrium, and assuming a closed system means only physically sealed rather than constant conditions with no matter exchange; and its permitted factual contextual examples are industrial chemical suppliers choosing temperature, pressure and catalysts by balancing equilibrium yield, rate, energy use and cost, and a sealed fizzy drink reaching equilibrium between dissolved and gaseous carbon dioxide until opening lowers the pressure.
- C25 has exact scope limited to explaining Haber-process conditions as a compromise and describing the production and uses of NPK fertilisers; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C19 equilibrium shifts and C16 acids and salts; its misconception checks target assuming the Haber process is the only industrial ammonia-related process worth naming, assuming higher pressure always gives a better outcome regardless of cost and safety, believing the iron catalyst is consumed during the process, and assuming a fertiliser's value comes from nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium alone with no other relevant factors; and its permitted factual contextual examples are race catering and global food supply depending on agriculture supported by ammonia fertilisers whose manufacture uses substantial energy, and garden fertiliser labels listing nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium because plants need each element for different growth functions.
- C33 has exact scope limited to measuring reaction rate by gas, mass or colour change and measuring energy changes with suitable controls and repeated observations; its prerequisite diagnostic checks C5 rate factors, C6 energy profiles, and M20 experimental uncertainty and repeatability; its misconception checks target treating rate as a single measurement rather than a quantity tracked over time, conflating a measured temperature change with the rate itself, assuming gas collection is always the fastest or best method regardless of the reaction, and expecting repeated trials to give identical rather than comparably close results; and its permitted factual contextual examples are fuel and materials testing using time-resolved measurements and thermal sensors with repeat runs needed to separate a real effect from measurement scatter, and comparing two curing products fairly by using equal masses, equal starting temperatures and the same insulated container.

## Tradeoffs And Risks

Reordering batches away from pure surface theme (moving C16 acids into Batch B) trades a slightly less thematically uniform batch for a strictly satisfied prerequisite chain, which was judged worth it because five later topics depend on acid chemistry and a forward reference would force a researcher to write esterification, ion-test, water-treatment, or salt-preparation prose before any acid content exists in the library to check against.
Coherent domain batches improve consistency and expose cross-topic contradictions, and per-topic review remains independent even though final product publication is subject-atomic to preserve catalog ordering and recoverability.
Two supporting resources is the minimum rather than a target for padding, so a third is retained only when it adds distinct high-quality value and every declared destination passes the same review.
The audit can establish connectivity and flag obvious gates but cannot prove chemical accuracy, safe advice, educational quality, model qualification, or true ungated access, so independent manual inspection remains mandatory.
Narrow or compound chemistry topics may lack one Wikipedia article covering every component, so a focused central article is acceptable only with an accurate label and no claim of complete topic coverage.
Some credible education sites vary content by region, cookies, scripts, or bot detection, so final browser inspection must use the learner-visible destination and unresolved access ambiguity must block the topic.
Chemistry practicals involve genuine hazards (corrosive reagents, chlorine gas from aqueous electrolysis, naked flames, hot apparatus) that physics and maths topics mostly do not, so the safety boundary in this plan is stricter than the physics plan's equivalent section and reviewers must treat it as a hard gate, not a style note.
F1 examples are engaging but especially prone to changing regulations, approximate figures, and hidden engineering assumptions, so only the manifest wording may be used unless separately inspected evidence supports a restrained explanation within scope.
